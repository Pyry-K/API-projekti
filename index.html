Barbarian - Barbarians are combination of melee attacks and raw damage make this warrior strong and scary to face against.

Bard - Was your dream to be a glamorous performer and a distiguised scholar, Bards seem to do it all.

Cleric - Clerics are versatile spellcasters, being able to heal their allies while also dealing damage to their foes.

Druid - Druid's love for nature allowes them to use healing magic or turn themselves into animals to fight for the nature.

Fighter - Fighters are highly skilled in the use of weapons and armor, making them a tough frontline in combat.

Monk - Monks are skilled in unarmed combat and can use their agility and speed to outmaneuver their opponents.

Paladin - Paladins are devoted to a sacred oath and use their divine powers to protect the innocent and smite evil.

Ranger - Rangers are skilled hunters and trackers, able to survive in the wild and take down their prey with a well planned ambush.

Rogue - Rogues are masters of stealth and deception, able to strike from the shadows, taking enemies down swiftly.

Sorcerer - Sorcerers are born with innate magical abilities, able to cast spells without the need for study or preparation.

Warlock - Warlocks gain their magical powers through a pact with an otherworldly being, granting them unique abilities and spells, for a price.

Wizard - Wizards are highly intelligent masters of knowledge, allowing them to cast a wide variety of spells to clear the way.
